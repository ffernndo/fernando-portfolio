Who is Fernando?

I'm Fernando, a specialist in turning complex data into visual, actionable strategies. Over 8 years at Unicred, I grew from operational to strategic roles, learning that the best results come from where rigorous analysis, smart design and clear communication meet.

My work connects three pillars: data that makes sense, design that persuades and strategy that works in the real world. Whether I'm building a dashboard that changes how your team works or developing a positioning that sets your brand apart, my goal is always measurable impact.

I don't believe in generic solutions. Every company is unique, with its own context, culture and challenges. That's why my process starts with deep listening. Then comes the analysis. And finally, execution that actually works.
